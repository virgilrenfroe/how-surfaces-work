How Brushed Metal Streaks

Most shiny surfaces spray reflections in a round blob. Brushed metal and satin have tiny grooves that stretch that blob into a streak.

Time 18 min
You will learn Anisotropy · brush angle · streak length
Try first Raise Anisotropy on Specimen 01
Core lesson Grooves stretch the highlight along one direction

What you will see

AnisotropyHow strongly grooves stretch a round highlight into a streak. Zero stays round.
Brush angleThe direction of the grooves. Turn it and the streak turns with it.
IsotropicA smooth chrome sphere keeps a round blob under the same light.

After the lab

Where you see this

Anisotropy is everyday craft for people who light brushed metal, satin devices, and hair.

  1. 01

    Brushed trim and hairline aluminum

    Automotive CGI artist

    Automotive CGI artists set anisotropy on brushed trim and hairline aluminum so highlights streak along the brush marks instead of blooming as round blobs on every panel.

  2. 02

    Watches, appliances, and satin finishes

    3D product visualization artist

    Product visualization artists dial anisotropy on watch bracelets, appliance doors, and satin bezels so studio lights draw long streaks that match real brushed stock.

  3. 03

    Hair and fur highlight streaks

    Look-development artist · groom artist

    Look-dev and groom artists align anisotropy with strand direction so hair catches light in thin bright lines. Without it, digital hair reads like shiny plastic tubes.

  4. 04

    Brushed vs polished metal on devices

    CMF designer

    CMF designers compare brushed and polished metal samples under the same lamp. Brushed stock keeps directional streaks; polished stock keeps rounder reflections that feel more mirror-like.

  5. 05

    Brushed steel railings and elevator panels

    Architectural visualization artist

    Archviz artists use anisotropy on brushed steel railings and elevator panels so lobby lights streak along the grain instead of spotting every surface with identical round highlights.

For teachers

Short objectives, one classroom loop, and how anisotropy maps to studio and design practice.

Learning objectives

  • Explain that anisotropy stretches a round highlight into a streak.
  • Predict that brush angle turns the streak with the grooves.
  • Contrast an isotropic chrome sphere with a brushed disc under one light.
  • Connect the same idea to satin weave and hair fibers.

10-minute classroom activity

1) Look at a brushed phone or laptop edge under a desk lamp. 2) Open Specimen 01 and match the streak direction. 3) On Specimen 02, write Disc aniso to zero and back. 4) Write one sentence on why the chrome sphere stayed round.

Art & design map

Automotive brushed trim, watch and appliance product shots, hair look-dev, CMF metal finish reviews.

Materials science map
