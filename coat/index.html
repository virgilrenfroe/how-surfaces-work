How Clear Coats Stack

Many real finishes are two layers: a colored base and a clear glossy coat on top. The coat adds a sharp mirror highlight of its own.

Time 18 min
You will learn Clearcoat · coat roughness · dual highlights

After the lab

Where you see this

Clearcoat is everyday craft for people who light car paint, gloss packaging, and lacquered props.

  1. 01

    Production car paint (base + clear)

    Automotive CGI artist

    Automotive CGI artists build production car paint as a colored base plus a clear lacquer. The coat carries the sharp showroom sparkle while the base holds the body color.

  2. 02

    Glossy devices and cosmetics packaging

    3D product visualization artist

    Product visualization artists use clearcoat on glossy phones, lipstick cases, and varnish-like packaging so studio lights make a crisp top highlight over the printed color.

  3. 03

    Soft-touch vs lacquer samples

    CMF designer

    CMF designers compare soft-touch and lacquer samples under the same lamp. Lacquer keeps a sharper coat highlight; soft-touch keeps a softer, more diffuse finish.

  4. 04

    Props with varnish and lacquer

    Look-development artist

    Look-dev artists dial clearcoat on furniture props, instruments, and lacquered wood so varnish reads as a thin glossy layer instead of a flat shiny color.

  5. 05

    Polished resin and glossy furniture

    Architectural visualization artist

    Archviz artists use clearcoat on polished resin counters and glossy furniture so lobby lights sparkle on the coat while the base color stays underneath.

For teachers

Short objectives, one classroom loop, and how clearcoat maps to studio and design practice.

Learning objectives

  • Explain that clearcoat is a glossy layer over a colored base.
  • Predict that lower coat roughness makes a sharper top highlight.
  • Contrast a matte base with the same base under lacquer.
  • Describe dual highlights: soft base plus sharp coat.

10-minute classroom activity

1) Look at a glossy phone or nail polish under a desk lamp. 2) Open Specimen 01 and raise Clearcoat. 3) Lower Coat roughness until the sparkle matches. 4) On Specimen 02, write one sentence on what the matte sphere is missing.
